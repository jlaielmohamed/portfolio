Barber Booking Platform

A booking site for a barber shop. Clients pick a service and a time slot, the appointment lands in the shop's Google Calendar automatically, and the owner runs the day from a dashboard.

  • Customer booking flow with service selection and live availability
  • Owner dashboard for appointments, services and daily schedule
  • Google Calendar API sync on every confirmed booking
  • Relational database behind services, slots and bookings

02 / About

A business student who would rather build the thing.

I study business administration at Tunis Business School, but most of what I know I taught myself — design in Figma, interfaces in Framer, and code in Python and JavaScript. I like the part where a messy manual process becomes a small, quiet piece of software.

Right now I'm building booking systems for local businesses and experimenting with n8n and AI agents to take the repetitive work out of the day — reminders, follow-ups, the things nobody has time to do.

I work small on purpose. A business with three employees doesn't need an enterprise platform; it needs one flow that works and a dashboard that makes sense on a phone.
